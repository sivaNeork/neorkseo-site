Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026

NeorkSEO is a Windows desktop app. It keeps its data on the PC where it runs. Neork Technologies does not receive your Google data or run a server that stores it.

Who this covers

This policy covers the NeorkSEO app and this website, both provided by Neork Technologies. "You" means the person or business running the app, and the staff they give access to. Whoever runs the app decides which sites and staff are added, and is responsible for that data on their PC.

Google data the app accesses

The app reads Google data only after you sign in to Google and allow it. You connect each tool separately, and access is read-only.

Service and permissionData read
Search Console
webmasters.readonly		The list of properties your account can open, and daily clicks, impressions and positions by query and page for the property you choose.
Google Analytics 4
analytics.readonly		The list of accounts and properties you can open, and daily sessions, engaged sessions, key events, channels and AI-assistant referral sources for the property you choose. These are totals, not records of individual visitors.

The app does not request your Google password, email, contacts or files. Google handles sign-in on its own pages.

PageSpeed Insights and the Chrome UX Report use an API key instead of your Google sign-in. The app sends them your site's address and receives public speed results.

How the data is used

Google data is used only to show you search and traffic reports for your sites inside the app. It is not used for advertising, sold, used to judge creditworthiness, or used to train AI models. The current version does not send Google user data to any AI service. If that changes, this policy will be updated and you will be asked to agree before the new use begins.

NeorkSEO's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other data the app keeps

  • Staff accounts: username, display name, role and a one-way hash of the password.
  • Sign-in sessions, notifications, and an audit log of sign-ins and changes.
  • Site details and preferences you enter, such as brand voice and competitors.

Storage and security

  • All of this is stored on the PC running the app.
  • Google sign-in tokens and keys are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The encryption key is protected by Windows.
  • Reports and other records are in a local database that the app does not itself encrypt. We recommend turning on Windows device encryption.
  • The app's service accepts connections only from the same PC, and connects to Google over HTTPS.

No method of storage is completely secure. Keep the PC updated and protect its Windows account.

Sharing

The app does not share Google user data with anyone. Staff you add can see data for the sites you assign to them. Requests to Google are handled under Google's Privacy Policy.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Disconnect a tool: in the app, choose Disconnect. This deletes the saved sign-in and asks Google to revoke it. Reports already collected stay on your PC.
  • Remove access at Google: you can do this at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Choose a different property: this deletes the reports collected from the previous one for that site.
  • Delete everything: close the app and its service, then delete the data folder, normally C:\ProgramData\NeorkSEO.

Data otherwise stays on the PC until you delete it. Archiving a site hides it but does not delete its data. Copies you make, such as your own backups, are yours to delete.

This website

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visitor information such as IP addresses. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

When you email us

If you contact us, we use your email address and message to reply and keep them only as long as needed for that or as the law requires.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to see, correct or delete your personal data. Neork Technologies does not hold app data, so staff should first ask the person or business that runs the app.
