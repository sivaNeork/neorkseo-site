Your websites' search and traffic data, on your own PC

NeorkSEO is a Windows desktop app by Neork Technologies for website owners and their teams. It reads data from the Google services you connect and shows it for each site in one place.

What it does

Search performance
Daily clicks, impressions and positions by search query and page, from Google Search Console.
Traffic
Sessions, engagement, key events, channels and visits from AI assistants, from Google Analytics 4.
Page speed
Lab results from PageSpeed Insights and real-user figures such as LCP, INP and CLS from the Chrome UX Report.
Team access
Staff accounts with roles, limited to the sites they are assigned.
Audit log
A record of sign-ins and changes that shows if it has been altered.

Why it asks for Google access

You choose which tools to connect for each site. NeorkSEO asks for read-only access and cannot change anything in your Search Console or Analytics account.

PermissionWhy it is needed
webmasters.readonlyList your Search Console properties and read the search performance of the one you choose.
analytics.readonlyList your GA4 properties and read the traffic reports of the one you choose.

Where your data stays

Data is stored on the PC where NeorkSEO runs. The app has no cloud account, and Neork Technologies does not receive your Google data. Google sign-ins are encrypted on that PC. The Privacy Policy has the details.

NeorkSEO is in development and is not available for public download yet.
